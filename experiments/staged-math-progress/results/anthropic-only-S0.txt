# S0: Task parsing and claim boundary

## Task Identity

**Experiment:** SV-COST-STAGED-001
**Problem class:** Logical characterization of an admissibility predicate for an abstract evaluator.

## Stipulated Abstract Evaluator

The evaluator is *abstract and stipulated for this experiment only*. It is not derived from, and makes no claims about, any deployed engine or implementation.

Primitive predicates (opaque, defined only by their names here):
- `GBP : Transition → Prop`
- `BD : Transition → Prop`
- `OC : Transition → Prop`

Evaluator output predicate:
- `ALLOW : Transition → Prop`

Stipulated rule (the *sole* definitional content):

> **Def-ALLOW:** For every candidate transition `t`, `ALLOW t ↔ GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t`.

## Claim Boundary

| In scope | Out of scope |
|---|---|
| Logical equivalence derivable from Def-ALLOW alone | Semantics of GBP, BD, OC beyond their types |
| Necessary and sufficient conditions as a biconditional | Any deployed GCAT/BCAT engine |
| Lean 4 statement of the logical core | Implementation validity |
| Abstract Lean 4 proof sketch | Correctness of any real transition system |

**Boundary statement (explicit):** No artifact in this experiment asserts anything about a deployed system. All claims are *purely logical consequences of Def-ALLOW as stipulated above*.

- **S2:** Lean 4 candidate for the logical core.
