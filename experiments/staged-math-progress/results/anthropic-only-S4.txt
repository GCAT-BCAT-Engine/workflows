# S4: Complete informal proof

## Theorem (N&S-ALLOW)

For every `t : Transition`:

```
ALLOW t  ↔  GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t
```

## Proof

### Preliminary

By **Def-ALLOW** (the sole stipulated rule), `ALLOW t` is by definition the proposition `GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t`. All reasoning below is purely logical unfolding of that definition under standard propositional logic (A1–A3, S1).

---

### Forward direction (Necessity): `ALLOW t → GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t`

Assume `ALLOW t`. By Def-ALLOW, this means exactly `GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t`. The conclusion is therefore identical to the hypothesis; no further reasoning is required.

Extracted individually:
- **GBP t** holds by projecting the first conjunct.
- **BD t** holds by projecting the second conjunct.
- **OC t** holds by projecting the third conjunct.

Each of GBP, BD, OC is thus *necessary* for ALLOW. □ (forward)

---

### Reverse direction (Sufficiency): `GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t → ALLOW t`

Assume `GBP t`, `BD t`, and `OC t` all hold. Form their conjunction `GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t`. By Def-ALLOW, this conjunction is exactly `ALLOW t`. Hence `ALLOW t` holds.

The triple conjunction is thus *sufficient* for ALLOW. □ (reverse)

---

### Biconditional conclusion

Both directions are established. Therefore:

```
ALLOW t  ↔  GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t
```

holds for every `t : Transition`. □

---

## Corollary (Failure-mode characterization)

**Claim:** `¬ ALLOW t  ↔  ¬ GBP t ∨ ¬ BD t ∨ ¬ OC t`

**Proof.**

By N&S-ALLOW, `¬ ALLOW t ↔ ¬(GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t)`. By De Morgan's law (classical propositional logic, A2):

```
¬(GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t)  ↔  ¬ GBP t ∨ ¬ BD t ∨ ¬ OC t
```

Chaining the two biconditionals yields the result. □

---

## Claim Boundary

| What is proved | What is not proved |
|---|---|
| Logical equivalence from Def-ALLOW alone | Any property of a deployed GCAT/BCAT engine |
| N&S biconditional for the abstract evaluator | Semantics of GBP, BD, OC beyond their types |
| Failure-mode corollary by De Morgan | Implementation validity or real transition correctness |

All results are purely logical consequences of the stipulated Definition ALLOW under assumptions A1–A3. No deployed-system claim is made or implied.
