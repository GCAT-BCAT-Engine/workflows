# S6: Verification ledger

## Purpose

This ledger records the verification status of every claim produced in S0–S5 against the four required evidence kinds: **verified**, **unresolved**, **evidence**, **implementation**.

---

## Evidence-Kind Definitions (for this ledger)

| Kind | Meaning in this experiment |
|---|---|
| **verified** | Claim follows from Def-ALLOW by logical steps that are complete and checkable |
| **unresolved** | Claim has a gap, ambiguity, or dependency that is not closed |
| **evidence** | The specific artifact (theorem name, tactic, or argument) that closes the claim |
| **implementation** | Whether any claim touches a deployed system (must be "none" throughout) |
