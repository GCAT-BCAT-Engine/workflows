S1: Definitions and assumptions.

- Let `Transition` be an abstract type of candidate transitions.
- Let `GBP BD OC ALLOW : Transition → Prop` be abstract predicates.
- Assume the evaluator contract:
  \[
  \forall t,\ \mathrm{ALLOW}(t)\leftrightarrow\bigl(\mathrm{GBP}(t)\land \mathrm{BD}(t)\land \mathrm{OC}(t)\bigr).
  \]
- No additional properties of `GBP`, `BD`, `OC`, or `ALLOW` are assumed.
- Scope: this assumption concerns only the stipulated abstract evaluator; it neither specifies a deployed engine nor claims implementation validity.
