S1: Definitions and assumptions.

Let `Transition` be an abstract type of candidate transitions. Define predicates:

- `GBP : Transition → Prop` — `GBP(t)` means candidate `t` satisfies the abstract GBP condition.
- `BD : Transition → Prop` — `BD(t)` means candidate `t` satisfies the abstract BD condition.
- `OC : Transition → Prop` — `OC(t)` means candidate `t` satisfies the abstract OC condition.
- `ALLOW : Transition → Prop` — `ALLOW(t)` means the stipulated abstract evaluator returns ALLOW for `t`.
- `Admissible : Transition → Prop` — define `Admissible(t) := GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t)`.

Assumption defining the evaluator:

\[
\forall t : Transition,\quad ALLOW(t) \leftrightarrow Admissible(t).
\]

Equivalently:

\[
\forall t,\quad ALLOW(t) \leftrightarrow \bigl(GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t)\bigr).
\]

These predicates are abstract and otherwise uninterpreted; no binding to or validity claim about any deployed GCAT/BCAT implementation is assumed.
