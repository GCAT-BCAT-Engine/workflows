S2: Theorem formulation.

**Theorem (ALLOW admissibility).** For every candidate transition `t`:
\[
\mathrm{ALLOW}(t)\iff \bigl(\mathrm{GBP}(t)\land \mathrm{BD}(t)\land \mathrm{OC}(t)\bigr).
\]

Thus `GBP(t)`, `BD(t)`, and `OC(t)` are jointly **necessary** and jointly **sufficient** for `ALLOW(t)`.

```lean
theorem allow_admissible
    {Transition : Type}
    (GBP BD OC ALLOW : Transition → Prop)
    (contract : ∀ t, ALLOW t ↔ (GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t))
    (t : Transition) :
    ALLOW t ↔ (GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t) :=
  contract t
```

**Claim boundary:** This theorem applies only under the stipulated abstract evaluator contract; it neither specifies a deployed engine nor claims implementation validity.
