S4: Complete informal proof.

Fix an arbitrary candidate transition \(t\).

- **Forward (necessity):** Assume \(\mathrm{ALLOW}(t)\). By the stipulated evaluator contract,
  \[
  \mathrm{ALLOW}(t)\leftrightarrow\bigl(\mathrm{GBP}(t)\land\mathrm{BD}(t)\land\mathrm{OC}(t)\bigr).
  \]
  Applying its forward implication yields
  \[
  \mathrm{GBP}(t)\land\mathrm{BD}(t)\land\mathrm{OC}(t).
  \]

- **Reverse (sufficiency):** Assume
  \[
  \mathrm{GBP}(t)\land\mathrm{BD}(t)\land\mathrm{OC}(t).
  \]
  Applying the reverse implication of the evaluator contract yields \(\mathrm{ALLOW}(t)\).

Therefore,
\[
\mathrm{ALLOW}(t)\iff\bigl(\mathrm{GBP}(t)\land\mathrm{BD}(t)\land\mathrm{OC}(t)\bigr).
\]
Since \(t\) was arbitrary, this holds for every candidate transition.

**Claim boundary:** The proof establishes only the logical characterization under the stipulated abstract evaluator contract. It neither specifies a deployed engine nor claims implementation validity.
