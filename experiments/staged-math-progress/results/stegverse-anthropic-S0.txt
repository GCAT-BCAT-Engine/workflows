# S0: Task parsing and claim boundary

---

## 1. Task Identity

**Experiment:** SV-COST-STAGED-001
**Goal:** Characterize the ALLOW admissibility predicate for an *abstract* GCAT/BCAT evaluator (not any deployed engine).
**Scope constraint:** No claim of conformance to any implementation is made or implied. The evaluator is stipulated purely by the definition below.

---

## 2. Stipulated Abstract Evaluator

**Primitive predicates** (opaque; no implementation semantics attached):

| Symbol | Reading |
|--------|---------|
| `GBP(t)` | "Good-Behavior Predicate holds for transition t" |
| `BD(t)` | "Boundary Disposition holds for transition t" |
| `OC(t)` | "Operational Condition holds for transition t" |

**Stipulated evaluation rule (SR):**

> For any candidate transition `t`,
>
> `ALLOW(t)  :⟺  GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t)`

This is a *definition*, not a derived theorem. It is the sole axiom of the abstract evaluator for this experiment.

---

## 3. Claim Boundary

### 3.1 Stipulated assumptions (axiomatic; not to be proved)

- **A1.** `GBP`, `BD`, `OC`, and `ALLOW` are predicates on a common type `T` of candidate transitions.
- **A2.** `ALLOW` is defined by SR (the conjunction above).

### 3.2 Claims to be established in later stages

- **C1 (Necessity).** `ALLOW(t) → GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t)`
- **C2 (Sufficiency).** `GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t) → ALLOW(t)`
- **C3 (Biconditional / N&S).** `ALLOW(t) ↔ GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t)`

*Note:* C1–C3 are trivially derivable from SR by unfolding the definition. Their value here is formal explicitness for downstream verification, not mathematical depth.

### 3.3 Generated claims (this stage)

- **G0.** The claim boundary is well-formed: A1–A2 are consistent (the conjunction of three arbitrary propositions is a coherent definition; no contradiction is introduced).

### 3.4 Unresolved implementation binding

- **U1.** Whether any deployed GCAT or BCAT engine satisfies SR is **not asserted and not in scope**. No evidence of conformance has been admitted or sought.
- **U2.** The semantic content of `GBP`, `BD`, `OC` relative to any real system remains unbound.
