# S1: Definitions and assumptions

---

## 1. Reused Artifacts

From S0 (admitted without modification):

- **Abstract evaluator stipulation:**
  `ALLOW(t) ↔ GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t)`
- **Claim boundary:** No deployed engine; no implementation conformance asserted.

---

## 2. Universe of Discourse

Let `T` be an arbitrary non-empty type whose elements are called **candidate transitions**. The variable `t` ranges over `T`.

No internal structure is imposed on `T`. No element of `T` is distinguished.

---

## 3. Primitive Predicates

The following three predicates are **uninterpreted** (opaque). No axioms govern their internal content beyond their type.

| Name | Type | Informal gloss |
|---|---|---|
| `GBP` | `T → Prop` | "Good-behavior predicate" |
| `BD` | `T → Prop` | "Boundary-condition predicate" |
| `OC` | `T → Prop` | "Operational-context predicate" |

**Assumption A-Opaque:** No relationship among `GBP`, `BD`, `OC` is assumed. In particular, neither pairwise implication nor independence is stipulated.

---

## 4. Defined Predicate

**Definition D-ALLOW** (stipulated; reproduced from S0):

> For all `t : T`,
> `ALLOW(t)` holds if and only if `GBP(t)` holds **and** `BD(t)` holds **and** `OC(t)` holds.

Formally:

```
∀ t : T,  ALLOW(t) ↔ GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t)
```

This is the **sole semantic axiom** of the abstract evaluator. It is a definition, not a theorem to be proved.

## 6. What Is Not Assumed

- No completeness, decidability, or computability of any predicate.
- No relationship between `T` and any concrete transition system.
- No deployed GCAT/BCAT engine behavior.
- No claim that `GBP`, `BD`, or `OC` are independent or exhaustive.
