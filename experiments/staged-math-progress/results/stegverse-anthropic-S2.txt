# S2: Theorem formulation

---

## 1. Reused Artifacts

From S0–S1 (admitted without modification):

- **D-ALLOW:** `∀ t : T, ALLOW(t) ↔ GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t)`
- **A-Opaque:** `GBP`, `BD`, `OC` are uninterpreted predicates on `T`.
- **Claim boundary:** Abstract evaluator only; no deployed engine; no implementation conformance.

---

## 2. Target Theorem

**Theorem T-NS (Necessary-and-Sufficient Characterization):**

> For every candidate transition `t : T`, `ALLOW(t)` holds if and only if each of `GBP(t)`, `BD(t)`, and `OC(t)` holds.

Formally:

```
∀ t : T,  ALLOW(t) ↔ GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t)
```

**Remark:** T-NS is *identical in form* to D-ALLOW. Its role here is to make explicit the two directed halves as named sub-claims (below) and to confirm that no hidden premises enter the characterization.

---

## 3. Directed Sub-Claims

### 3.1 Sufficiency (S→)

**Claim T-NS-S (Sufficiency):**

> For all `t : T`, if `GBP(t)` and `BD(t)` and `OC(t)` all hold, then `ALLOW(t)` holds.

```
∀ t : T,  GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t) → ALLOW(t)
```

*Derivation sketch:* Immediate from the right-to-left direction of D-ALLOW. No additional premises are required.

---

### 3.2 Necessity (N←)

**Claim T-NS-N (Necessity):**

> For all `t : T`, if `ALLOW(t)` holds, then `GBP(t)` holds **and** `BD(t)` holds **and** `OC(t)` holds.

```
∀ t : T,  ALLOW(t) → GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t)
```

*Derivation sketch:* Immediate from the left-to-right direction of D-ALLOW. No additional premises are required.

**Corollaries of necessity (named for evidence requirements):**

| Label | Statement |
|---|---|
| T-NS-N-GBP | `∀ t, ALLOW(t) → GBP(t)` |
| T-NS-N-BD | `∀ t, ALLOW(t) → BD(t)` |
| T-NS-N-OC | `∀ t, ALLOW(t) → OC(t)` |

Each corollary follows by conjunction elimination from T-NS-N.

---
