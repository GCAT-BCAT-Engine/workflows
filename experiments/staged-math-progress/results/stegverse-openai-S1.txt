S1: Definitions and assumptions

**Abstract setting**
- Let \(T\) be the type of candidate transitions.
- Let \(GBP, BD, OC : T \to \mathrm{Prop}\).
- Let \(\operatorname{Eval} : T \to \{\mathrm{ALLOW},\mathrm{DENY}\}\).

**Stipulated evaluator assumption**
For every \(t : T\),
\[
\operatorname{Eval}(t)=\mathrm{ALLOW}
\iff
GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t).
\]

Thus `ALLOW` is stipulated exactly when `GBP`, `BD`, and `OC` all hold.

**Claim boundary**
These definitions concern only the abstract evaluator. They provide no deployed GCAT/BCAT specification, conformance result, or implementation-validity claim.
