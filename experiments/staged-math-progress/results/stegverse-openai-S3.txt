S3: Proof strategy and lemma decomposition

**Proof strategy**

Fix \(t : T\). Instantiate the stipulated evaluator equivalence:
\[
\operatorname{Eval}(t)=\mathrm{ALLOW}
\iff
GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t).
\]

1. **Forward / necessity lemma**
   \[
   \operatorname{Eval}(t)=\mathrm{ALLOW}
   \to GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t).
   \]
   Obtain this using the forward implication of the stipulated equivalence.

2. **Reverse / sufficiency lemma**
   \[
   GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t)
   \to \operatorname{Eval}(t)=\mathrm{ALLOW}.
   \]
   Obtain this using the reverse implication of the stipulated equivalence.

3. **Characterization theorem**
   Combine the necessity and sufficiency lemmas with biconditional introduction to recover:
   \[
   \operatorname{Eval}(t)=\mathrm{ALLOW}
   \iff GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t).
   \]

**Lean-oriented decomposition**

Use one hypothesis for the stipulated equivalence, derive the two directions via `.mp` and `.mpr`, and construct the final `Iff` with `Iff.intro`. No classical reasoning or deployed-engine assumptions are required.

**Claim boundary**

This strategy proves only the propositional result for the stipulated abstract evaluator. It establishes no deployed GCAT/BCAT specification, conformance, behavior, or implementation validity.
