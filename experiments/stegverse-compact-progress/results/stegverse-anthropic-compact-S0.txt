## Task Parse

**Problem domain:** Abstract logical characterization of an ALLOW decision in a stylized evaluator (referred to here as the GCAT/BCAT evaluator). No deployed implementation is referenced.

**Given:**
- Three predicates on a candidate transition *t*:
  - `GBP(t)` — "good base predicate" (name stipulated, content uninterpreted)
  - `BD(t)` — "boundary/domain predicate" (name stipulated, content uninterpreted)
  - `OC(t)` — "operational condition predicate" (name stipulated, content uninterpreted)
- Evaluator output rule (stipulated): `ALLOW(t) ↔ GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t)`

**Task decomposition:**
1. Establish the necessary-and-sufficient (iff) characterization of ALLOW admissibility.
2. State the claim boundary precisely.
3. Prepare a Lean 4 candidate for the logical core.
