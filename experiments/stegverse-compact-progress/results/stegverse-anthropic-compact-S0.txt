**Domain framing (stipulated):**
The problem concerns an *abstract* evaluator, notionally labeled GCAT/BCAT. No claim is made that any deployed GCAT/BCAT system is described or constrained by what follows. All predicates and the ALLOW relation are abstract symbols.

**Given primitives (stipulated definitions, this stage only):**

| Symbol | Type | Role |
|--------|------|------|
| `GBP` | predicate on candidate transitions | "Good-basis predicate" (name stipulated) |
| `BD` | predicate on candidate transitions | "Boundary predicate" (name stipulated) |
| `OC` | predicate on candidate transitions | "Other condition" (name stipulated) |
| `ALLOW` | predicate on candidate transitions | Output relation of the evaluator |

**Evaluator rule (stipulated, source: problem statement):**

> The evaluator returns ALLOW exactly when GBP and BD and OC all hold.

This is a biconditional specification:

$$\forall t,\quad \mathrm{ALLOW}(t) \iff \mathrm{GBP}(t) \land \mathrm{BD}(t) \land \mathrm{OC}(t)$$
