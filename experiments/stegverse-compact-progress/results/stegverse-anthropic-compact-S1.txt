S1: Definitions and assumptions

---

## 1. Purpose

This artifact establishes the formal definitions and assumptions required to support the necessary-and-sufficient characterization of `ALLOW` in Stage S2. All items below are **stipulations** for this experiment. No item asserts conformance of any deployed system.

---

## 2. Universe of Discourse

**DEF-U.** Let `C` be an arbitrary type (the *candidate type*). Elements of `C` are called *candidate transitions*. No internal structure of `C` is assumed.

---

## 3. Primitive Predicates

The following three predicates are taken as primitive — uninterpreted functions from candidates to propositions:

| Identifier | Type | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| `GBP` | `C → Prop` | "Governed Boundary Predicate holds on c" |
| `BD` | `C → Prop` | "Boundary Determination holds on c" |
| `OC` | `C → Prop` | "Operational Condition holds on c" |

**DEF-PRIM.** `GBP`, `BD`, and `OC` are stipulated as total predicates on `C`. No relationship among them is assumed; they may be independent, coincident, or contradictory on any particular `c`. No decidability assumption is made.

---

## 4. The ALLOW Predicate

**DEF-ALLOW (S-DEF).** For all `c : C`,

```
ALLOW(c)  :=  GBP(c) ∧ BD(c) ∧ OC(c)
```

This definition is the **sole axiomatic stipulation** governing `ALLOW` in this experiment. It is not derived; it is constitutive.

**Remark (S-DEF scope).** S-DEF characterizes the abstract evaluator model only. It does not assert that any deployed GCAT/BCAT implementation computes `ALLOW` by this rule, nor that `GBP`, `BD`, `OC` correspond to any concrete runtime checks.

---

## 5. Structural Assumptions

**ASM-CLASSIC.** The ambient logic is classical propositional/predicate logic (or, in the Lean 4 formalization, the Prop-valued fragment of the Calculus of Constructions with classical axioms available but not required for the core conjunction).

**ASM-TOTAL.** All three predicates are total: for every `c : C`, each of `GBP(c)`, `BD(c)`, `OC(c)` is either provably true or provably false under any complete valuation. (This assumption is used only to support later case analysis; it is not required for the definitional claim itself.)

**ASM-INDEPENDENCE.** No logical dependency among `GBP`, `BD`, `OC` is assumed. They are treated as three independent atomic propositions for any given `c`.

**ASM-NO-IMPL.** No assumption is made that any software artifact evaluates these predicates. The model is purely abstract.

---
