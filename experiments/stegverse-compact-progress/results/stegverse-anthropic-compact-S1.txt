S1: Definitions and assumptions

---

## 1. Universe and Primitive Sorts

**Sort.**
Let `T` be an arbitrary non-empty type, whose elements are called *candidate transitions*. No structure is placed on `T` beyond its being a type; in particular, no topology, ordering, or algebraic operations are assumed.

**Variables.**
Throughout, `t : T` ranges over candidate transitions.

---

## 2. Primitive Predicates

The following three predicates are introduced as **abstract symbols**. They carry no definition beyond their types; all semantic content is supplied by the problem statement and by C1 below.

| Symbol | Type | Informal gloss |
|--------|------|----------------|
| `GBP` | `T → Prop` | "Good-Behavior Predicate" — abstract condition on `t` |
| `BD` | `T → Prop` | "Boundary/Domain" — abstract condition on `t` |
| `OC` | `T → Prop` | "Output Condition" — abstract condition on `t` |

**Assumption A-Pred.**
`GBP`, `BD`, and `OC` are total predicates on `T`. For every `t : T`, each of `GBP t`, `BD t`, `OC t` is a well-formed proposition (possibly false, possibly true; no default is assumed).

---

## 3. The ALLOW Relation

**Definition D-ALLOW (stipulated).**
`ALLOW : T → Prop` is defined by the biconditional

$$\forall t : T,\quad \mathrm{ALLOW}(t) \;\iff\; \mathrm{GBP}(t) \;\land\; \mathrm{BD}(t) \;\land\; \mathrm{OC}(t)$$

This is **definitional**, not derived. It records the problem statement as a formal stipulation. No proof obligation arises from it beyond admitting it as an axiom within the abstract theory.

---

## 4. Derived Directions (admitted, not yet proved)

From D-ALLOW, two directions are immediately named for downstream use:

| Label | Statement | Status |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| **C1** | `∀ t, ALLOW t ↔ GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t` | **Stipulated** — definitional |
| **C2 (necessity)** | `∀ t, ALLOW t → GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t` | Admitted; follows from C1 by `Iff.mp` |
| **C3 (sufficiency)** | `∀ t, GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t → ALLOW t` | Admitted; follows from C1 by `Iff.mpr` |

C2 and C3 are **not independently verified** at this stage; they are marked *admitted pending S2 proof obligations*.

---

## 5. Explicit Non-Assumptions

The following are **not** assumed and must not be introduced in later stages without explicit admission:

- `GBP`, `BD`, `OC` are not assumed independent of one another.
- No implication among `GBP`, `BD`, `OC` is assumed (e.g., `GBP t → BD t` is not stipulated).
- `T` is not assumed finite, countable, or inhabited by any particular element.
- No claim is made that any deployed GCAT/BCAT system satisfies D-ALLOW.
