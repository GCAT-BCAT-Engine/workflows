## 2. Theorem Statement

**Theorem T-ALLOW-NAS** *(Necessary-and-Sufficient Characterization of ALLOW)*

Let `T` be an arbitrary type. Let `GBP`, `BD`, `OC`, `ALLOW : T → Prop` satisfy D-ALLOW. Then for every `t : T`:

$$\mathrm{ALLOW}(t) \;\iff\; \mathrm{GBP}(t) \;\land\; \mathrm{BD}(t) \;\land\; \mathrm{OC}(t)$$

That is:

- **(Sufficiency / Forward direction):** If `GBP(t)` and `BD(t)` and `OC(t)`, then `ALLOW(t)`.
- **(Necessity / Backward direction):** If `ALLOW(t)`, then `GBP(t)` and `BD(t)` and `OC(t)`.

**Corollaries** (immediate consequences, not separately named):

- C1. `¬ALLOW(t) ↔ ¬GBP(t) ∨ ¬BD(t) ∨ ¬OC(t)`
- C2. Each of `GBP`, `BD`, `OC` is individually necessary for `ALLOW`.
- C3. Their joint satisfaction is sufficient and exhaustive.
