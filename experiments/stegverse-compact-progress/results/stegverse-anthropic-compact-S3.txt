## 2. Proof Strategy

**THM-ALLOW-IFF (S2-MAIN)** asserts:

> `ALLOW(c) ↔ GBP(c) ∧ BD(c) ∧ OC(c)`

Since `ALLOW` is defined by DEF-ALLOW (S-DEF) as the conjunction `GBP(c) ∧ BD(c) ∧ OC(c)`, the biconditional holds by **definitional unfolding followed by reflexivity of `↔`**. No induction, case analysis, or auxiliary hypotheses are required. The proof is a one-step rewrite under the definition.

**Chosen proof method:** `unfold ALLOW` (or `simp only [ALLOW]`) followed by `exact Iff.rfl`.

This is the minimal and complete strategy. The theorem is not empirically contingent; it is a logical consequence of the stipulated definition alone.

## 3. Lemma Decomposition

Although the main theorem requires no non-trivial lemmas, we decompose the biconditional into its two directional components for explicitness and for use as named sub-lemmas in the Lean 4 candidate (S4).

### LEM-FWD (Necessity)

> If `ALLOW(c)` then `GBP(c) ∧ BD(c) ∧ OC(c)`.

**Proof sketch:** By DEF-ALLOW, `ALLOW(c)` is defined as `GBP(c) ∧ BD(c) ∧ OC(c)`. Unfolding the definition yields the conclusion directly. No premises beyond the definition are used.

**Status:** Stipulated sub-lemma. Provable by `intro h; exact h` after unfolding.

---

### LEM-BWD (Sufficiency)

> If `GBP(c) ∧ BD(c) ∧ OC(c)` then `ALLOW(c)`.

**Proof sketch:** By DEF-ALLOW, `ALLOW(c)` unfolds to `GBP(c) ∧ BD(c) ∧ OC(c)`. The hypothesis is exactly the conclusion after unfolding. No premises beyond the definition are used.

**Status:** Stipulated sub-lemma. Provable by `intro h; exact h` after unfolding.

---

### LEM-CONJ-COMM (Auxiliary, optional)

> `GBP(c) ∧ BD(c) ∧ OC(c)` is equivalent under any permutation of conjuncts.

**Status:** Not required for the main proof. Noted for completeness; associativity and commutativity of `∧` in Prop are standard in Lean 4 / Mathlib. Not used in the minimal proof path.
