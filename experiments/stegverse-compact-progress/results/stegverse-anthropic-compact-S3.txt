## 3. Proof Strategy

### 3.1 High-Level Approach

T-ALLOW-NAS asserts a biconditional. The canonical strategy is:

> Prove (→) and (←) separately, then combine with `Iff.intro`.

Because `ALLOW` is stipulated as the conjunction (D-ALLOW), both directions reduce to unfolding the definition and applying the identity of the conjunction.

**Forward direction (necessity):**
`ALLOW(t) → GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t)`
Unfold D-ALLOW; the hypothesis immediately yields the conjunction.

**Backward direction (sufficiency):**
`GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t) → ALLOW(t)`
Fold D-ALLOW; the conjunction is exactly the definiens.

### 3.2 Proof Mode

In Lean 4, if `ALLOW` is introduced via `def` or `abbrev` equating it to the conjunction, `simp` / `exact` / `constructor` suffice. If introduced via an `axiom` or `opaque` plus a separate axiom schema, we need to cite that axiom explicitly.

The Lean 4 candidate (Stage S4) will treat both cases; here we record the lemma decomposition only.
