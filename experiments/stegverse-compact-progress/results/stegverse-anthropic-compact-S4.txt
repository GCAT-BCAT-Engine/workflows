### 1. Restatement of What Is to Be Proved

**THM-ALLOW-IFF (S2-MAIN).**
For all candidates `c : C`,

```
ALLOW(c) ↔ GBP(c) ∧ BD(c) ∧ OC(c)
```

---

### 2. Definitions in Force (from S1)

- **DEF-ALLOW:** `ALLOW(c)` is defined to mean `GBP(c) ∧ BD(c) ∧ OC(c)`.
- `GBP`, `BD`, `OC` are arbitrary predicates `C → Prop`.
- No axioms beyond DEF-ALLOW are required.

---

### 3. Proof of the Forward Direction (LEM-FWD: Necessity)

**Claim:** `ALLOW(c) → GBP(c) ∧ BD(c) ∧ OC(c)`

**Proof.**
Assume `ALLOW(c)` holds. By DEF-ALLOW, `ALLOW(c)` is *identical by definition* to `GBP(c) ∧ BD(c) ∧ OC(c)`. Therefore the assumption directly yields `GBP(c) ∧ BD(c) ∧ OC(c)`. The hypothesis is discharged without any additional reasoning. □

**Proof method:** Definitional unfolding followed by assumption discharge (`intro h; exact h` in term mode).

---

### 4. Proof of the Backward Direction (LEM-BWD: Sufficiency)

**Claim:** `GBP(c) ∧ BD(c) ∧ OC(c) → ALLOW(c)`

**Proof.**
Assume `GBP(c) ∧ BD(c) ∧ OC(c)` holds. By DEF-ALLOW, `ALLOW(c)` is defined to be precisely this conjunction. Therefore `ALLOW(c)` holds immediately by folding the definition. □

**Proof method:** Definitional folding (`intro h; exact h` in term mode, symmetrically to LEM-FWD).

---

### 5. Assembly of THM-ALLOW-IFF

**Proof.**
The biconditional `ALLOW(c) ↔ GBP(c) ∧ BD(c) ∧ OC(c)` is established by introducing both directions:

- The forward direction is LEM-FWD (§3).
- The backward direction is LEM-BWD (§4).

Since DEF-ALLOW makes `ALLOW(c)` *definitionally equal* to `GBP(c) ∧ BD(c) ∧ OC(c)`, the biconditional is in fact an instance of `P ↔ P`, i.e., `Iff.rfl` in Lean 4's type theory. Both directions reduce to identity; no case analysis, induction, or auxiliary lemma with non-trivial content is required.

The proof is complete. □
