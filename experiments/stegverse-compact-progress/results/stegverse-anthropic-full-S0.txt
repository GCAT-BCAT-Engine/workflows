## 1. Task Parsing

**Experiment identifier:** SV-COST-STAGED-001
**Capability test:** SV-COMPACT-PROGRESS-001

**Object of study:**
An abstract evaluator (referred to as a GCAT/BCAT evaluator) that operates on candidate transitions. The evaluator produces a binary output: ALLOW or (implicitly) DENY.

**Given predicates (stipulated, not derived):**
- `GBP` — a predicate on a candidate transition
- `BD` — a predicate on a candidate transition
- `OC` — a predicate on a candidate transition

**Stipulated semantics of the evaluator:**
> The evaluator returns ALLOW on a candidate transition `t` if and only if `GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t)`.

This is a *stipulation* introduced by the task. It is not a claim about any deployed system.

## 3. Necessary and Sufficient Form (Pre-formal Statement)

The characterization to be established across subsequent stages is:

> **Claim C1 (informal):** For the abstract evaluator defined above, a candidate transition `t` satisfies `ALLOW(t)` if and only if all three of `GBP(t)`, `BD(t)`, and `OC(t)` hold.

- **Necessary direction:** If `ALLOW(t)`, then `GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t)`.
- **Sufficient direction:** If `GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t)`, then `ALLOW(t)`.

Both directions follow directly from the stipulated definition. The logical work in subsequent stages is to make this precise in a formal language (Lean 4) and to separate definitional from derived content.
