**Subject of characterization:** An *abstract* GCAT/BCAT evaluator — a formal object defined solely by the stipulations below. No deployed implementation is referenced.

**Primitive predicates (stipulated, not defined externally):**

| Symbol | Type | Role |
|--------|------|------|
| `GBP` | `Candidate → Prop` | "Good-Bid Predicate" (name is stipulated only) |
| `BD` | `Candidate → Prop` | "Bid-Dominant" (name is stipulated only) |
