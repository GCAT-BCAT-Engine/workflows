## 2. Claim Boundary

```
ALLOW(t) ↔ GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t)
```

for an arbitrary candidate transition `t`.

### 2.3 Scope of the biconditional

The biconditional is purely propositional (classical logic, no modal operators, no quantifier structure beyond ranging over candidate transitions). Its proof obligation reduces to two directions:

- **(Sufficiency / →):** If `GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t)` then `ALLOW(t)`
- **(Necessity / ←):** If `ALLOW(t)` then `GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t)`

Both directions are direct consequences of the stipulated rule. No lemmas from external libraries are required beyond propositional logic.
