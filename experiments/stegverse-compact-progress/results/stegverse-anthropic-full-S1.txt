## S1 Artifact: Definitions and Assumptions

---

### 1. Universe of Discourse

**DEF-1 (Candidate Transition Type).**
Let `T` be an arbitrary non-empty type. Elements `t : T` are called *candidate transitions*. No further structure is imposed on `T`.

**DEF-2 (Predicates).**
The following are stipulated as total, uninterpreted predicates on `T`:

| Name | Type | Role |
|------|------|------|
| `GBP` | `T → Prop` | First gate predicate |
| `BD` | `T → Prop` | Second gate predicate |
| `OC` | `T → Prop` | Third gate predicate |

"Uninterpreted" means: no axiom governs the truth value of `GBP(t)`, `BD(t)`, or `OC(t)` for any particular `t`. They are free in the logical environment.

**DEF-3 (ALLOW).**
The evaluator output predicate `ALLOW : T → Prop` is defined by:

```
ALLOW(t)  :=  GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t)
```

This definition is stipulated, not derived. It is the sole semantic content of the evaluator specification.

---

### 2. Logical Setting

**ASM-1 (Classical or Intuitionistic compatibility).**
The characterization holds in both classical and intuitionistic propositional logic. No excluded-middle axiom is required because the target claim is a biconditional whose right-to-left and left-to-right directions are each constructive.

**ASM-2 (Conjunction semantics).**
`∧` denotes standard binary conjunction. The conjunction `GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t)` is parsed as `GBP(t) ∧ (BD(t) ∧ OC(t))` (right-associative), which is propositionally equivalent to any other bracketing and to the flat three-way conjunction.

**ASM-3 (Universality).**
The characterization is asserted for *all* `t : T` by universal quantification. No distinguished or witness element of `T` is assumed.

**ASM-4 (No side conditions).**
The evaluator has no implicit preconditions, error states, or partial definedness. `ALLOW` is total on `T`.
