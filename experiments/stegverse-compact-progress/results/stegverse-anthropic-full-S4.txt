### 2. Preliminary Remarks on Proof Character

The proof is **analytic** in the strict sense: it contains no synthetic steps, introduces no auxiliary objects, and requires no induction, case analysis, or appeal to external lemmas. Both directions of the biconditional reduce immediately to the observation that `ALLOW(t)` and `GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t)` are *definitionally identical* expressions (DEF-3). The entire mathematical content of THEOREM-1 is the act of making this identity explicit and structured.

The proof is recorded in full nonetheless, for three reasons:
1. To discharge PO-1 and PO-2 as formal proof obligations with named steps.
2. To provide the informal ground for the Lean 4 candidate (next stage).
3. To maintain the staged artifact discipline required by the experiment.
