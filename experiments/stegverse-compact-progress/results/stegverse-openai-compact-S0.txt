**Task identity**  
Characterize ALLOW admissibility for an abstract GCAT/BCAT evaluator using predicates `GBP`, `BD`, and `OC` on a candidate transition.

**Stipulations**
- A candidate transition is evaluated by three predicates: `GBP`, `BD`, and `OC`.
- The abstract evaluator returns `ALLOW` exactly when all three predicates hold.
- No admitted artifacts are available.

**Target claim**
For every candidate transition `t`:

`ALLOW(t) ↔ (GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t))`.

This is the intended necessary-and-sufficient characterization:
- Necessity: `ALLOW(t)` implies `GBP(t)`, `BD(t)`, and `OC(t)`.
- Sufficiency: their conjunction implies `ALLOW(t)`.

**Claim boundary**
- The claim concerns only the stipulated abstract evaluator.
- It does not establish that any deployed or concrete GCAT/BCAT implementation uses these predicates, computes them correctly, or conforms to this characterization.
- A later Lean 4 candidate may encode only the logical core; compilation and implementation validity remain separate evidence obligations.
