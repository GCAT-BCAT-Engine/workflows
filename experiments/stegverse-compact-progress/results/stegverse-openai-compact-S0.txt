S0: Task parsing and claim boundary.

**Task identity**  
Characterize ALLOW admissibility for an abstract GCAT/BCAT evaluator using predicates `GBP`, `BD`, and `OC` on a candidate transition.

**Stipulated semantics**  
For every candidate transition `t`, the abstract evaluator returns `ALLOW` exactly when all three predicates hold:
- `GBP t`
- `BD t`
- `OC t`

**Target logical claim**  
\[
\operatorname{ALLOW}(t)\iff GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t).
\]

This is the required necessary-and-sufficient characterization:
- Necessity: `ALLOW t → GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t`.
- Sufficiency: `GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t → ALLOW t`.

**Claim boundary**  
The claim concerns only the stipulated abstract evaluator and its logical specification. It does not establish that any deployed or concrete GCAT/BCAT implementation computes these predicates correctly, uses this decision rule, or conforms to the characterization.
