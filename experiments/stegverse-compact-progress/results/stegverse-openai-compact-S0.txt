**Task identity**  
Experiment `SV-COST-STAGED-001`; capability test `SV-COMPACT-PROGRESS-001`.

**Stipulations**  
For an abstract candidate transition \(t\), let \(GBP(t)\), \(BD(t)\), and \(OC(t)\) be predicates. The evaluator returns `ALLOW` exactly when all three predicates hold.

**Target claim for later establishment**  
\[
\operatorname{ALLOW}(t)\iff GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t).
\]

Equivalently, `ALLOW` is admissible precisely when each of `GBP`, `BD`, and `OC` holds; failure of any one makes `ALLOW` inadmissible.

**Claim boundary**  
The target is only the propositional characterization of the stipulated abstract evaluator. No claim is made about the meaning, correctness, completeness, or computability of the predicates, nor about any concrete or deployed GCAT/BCAT implementation. A later Lean 4 candidate may encode the logical core, but compilation, proof acceptance, integration, and implementation conformance remain unverified.
