S1: Definitions and assumptions.

### Stipulations

Let `Transition` be an arbitrary type of candidate transitions, with predicates:

- `GBP : Transition → Prop`
- `BD : Transition → Prop`
- `OC : Transition → Prop`
- `ALLOW : Transition → Prop`

Assume the abstract evaluator specification:

\[
\forall t,\quad \operatorname{ALLOW}(t)
\leftrightarrow
\bigl(GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t)\bigr).
\]

No assumptions are made about decidability, independence, or the internal meanings of `GBP`, `BD`, and `OC`.

### Generated claims

For each transition `t`, the specification is intended to yield:

- Necessity:
  \[
  \operatorname{ALLOW}(t)\rightarrow GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t).
  \]
- Sufficiency:
  \[
  GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t)\rightarrow \operatorname{ALLOW}(t).
  \]

These claims are inherited from the admitted S0 artifact; no new proof is supplied at this stage.

### Claim boundary and unresolved evidence

The specification concerns only the abstract predicates above. No claim is made that any deployed or concrete GCAT/BCAT evaluator implements or conforms to it. The binding between these predicates and any implementation remains unresolved.
