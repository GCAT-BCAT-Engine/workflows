S1: Definitions and assumptions.

**Stipulations**

- `Transition` is an abstract type of candidate transitions.
- `GBP`, `BD`, and `OC` are predicates on `Transition`.
- `ALLOW` is the abstract evaluator’s allow-outcome predicate.
- Evaluator specification:
  \[
  \forall t,\quad \operatorname{ALLOW}(t)\leftrightarrow
  \bigl(\operatorname{GBP}(t)\land \operatorname{BD}(t)\land \operatorname{OC}(t)\bigr).
  \]

**Lean 4 candidate context**

```lean
universe u

variable {Transition : Type u}

def Conditions
    (GBP BD OC : Transition → Prop)
    (t : Transition) : Prop :=
  GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t

variable (GBP BD OC ALLOW : Transition → Prop)

variable
  (evaluator_spec :
    ∀ t, ALLOW t ↔ Conditions GBP BD OC t)
```

**Generated claims for later proof**

- Necessity: `ALLOW t` implies `GBP t`, `BD t`, and `OC t`.
- Sufficiency: `GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t` implies `ALLOW t`.
- Hence `ALLOW t` is equivalent to `Conditions GBP BD OC t`.

**Verified claims**

- The admitted S0 artifact fixes the abstract characterization and excludes claims about concrete or deployed implementations.
- No Lean compilation or proof has been verified at this stage.

**Claim boundary and unresolved evidence**

The specification concerns only the abstract evaluator described above. No concrete GCAT/BCAT evaluator is identified, inspected, or shown to satisfy `evaluator_spec`; implementation validity and conformance remain unresolved.
