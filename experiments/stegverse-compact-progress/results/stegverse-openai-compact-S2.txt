S2: Theorem formulation.

**Stipulations**

For an arbitrary type `Transition` and predicates

- `GBP : Transition → Prop`,
- `BD : Transition → Prop`,
- `OC : Transition → Prop`,

define:

\[
\operatorname{ALLOW}(t) \;:\!\iff\; GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t).
\]

No additional properties are assumed.

**Generated theorem claim**

For every candidate transition \(t\):

\[
\operatorname{ALLOW}(t)\iff GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t).
\]

Equivalently:

- **Necessity:** \(\operatorname{ALLOW}(t)\to GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t)\).
- **Sufficiency:** \(GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t)\to\operatorname{ALLOW}(t)\).

**Lean 4 candidate for the logical core**

  GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t

This formulation concerns only the stipulated abstract evaluator and its propositional definition of `ALLOW`. It does not establish that any deployed or external GCAT/BCAT evaluator uses these predicates, computes this conjunction, or otherwise conforms to the model.
