S2: Theorem formulation.

### Stipulations

Let:

- `Transition` be an arbitrary type.
- `GBP BD OC ALLOW : Transition → Prop`.
- The abstract evaluator satisfy:
  \[
  \forall t,\quad ALLOW(t)\leftrightarrow
  \bigl(GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t)\bigr).
  \]

No decidability, independence, or internal interpretation of the predicates is assumed.

### Generated claims

For every candidate transition \(t\):

\[
ALLOW(t)\iff GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t).
\]

Equivalently:

- **Necessity**
  \[
  ALLOW(t)\rightarrow GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t).
  \]

- **Sufficiency**
  \[
  GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t)\rightarrow ALLOW(t).
  \]

Thus `ALLOW` is admissible exactly when all three predicates hold.
