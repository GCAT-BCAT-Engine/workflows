S3: Proof strategy and lemma decomposition.

**Admitted stipulation**

For arbitrary `Transition` and predicates `GBP BD OC : Transition → Prop`:

\[
\operatorname{ALLOW}(t) := GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t).
\]

No additional properties are assumed.

**Proof strategy**

1. **Necessity:** unfold `ALLOW`; an `ALLOW t` proof is already a proof of `GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t`.
2. **Sufficiency:** unfold `ALLOW`; a proof of `GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t` directly proves `ALLOW t`.
3. **Characterization:** combine necessity and sufficiency to obtain
   \[
   \operatorname{ALLOW}(t)\iff GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t).
   \]

**Lemma decomposition**

- `allow_necessary`:
  \[
  \operatorname{ALLOW}(t)\to GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t).
  \]
- `allow_sufficient`:
  \[
  GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t)\to\operatorname{ALLOW}(t).
  \]
- `allow_iff`: packages both directions as the necessary-and-sufficient characterization.
