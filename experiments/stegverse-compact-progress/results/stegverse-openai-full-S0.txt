### Stipulated model
For an arbitrary candidate transition `t`:

- `GBP t`, `BD t`, and `OC t` are propositions.
- The abstract evaluator returns `ALLOW` exactly when all three propositions hold.

Formally, the governing stipulation is:

\[
\operatorname{ALLOW}(t) \iff GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t).
\]

### Required result
Establish the necessary-and-sufficient characterization:

- **Necessity:** `ALLOW t` implies `GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t`.
- **Sufficiency:** `GBP t ∧ BD t ∧ OC t` implies `ALLOW t`.

A later stage may encode this logical core as a Lean 4 candidate.

### Claim boundary
The result concerns only the stipulated abstract evaluator and its predicates. It does **not** establish:

- correctness or conformity of any deployed GCAT/BCAT implementation;
- correspondence between `GBP`, `BD`, or `OC` and implementation checks;
- soundness of predicate computation;
- validity of a Lean 4 artifact before type-checking in a specified environment.
