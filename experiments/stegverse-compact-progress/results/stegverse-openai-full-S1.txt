**Definitions**
Let `Transition` be an arbitrary type of candidate transitions. Let:
- `GBP : Transition → Prop`
- `BD : Transition → Prop`
- `OC : Transition → Prop`
- `ALLOW : Transition → Prop`

Here, `ALLOW(t)` means that the stipulated abstract evaluator returns `ALLOW` for transition `t`.

**Assumption**
The evaluator’s stipulated decision rule is:

`∀ t : Transition, ALLOW(t) ↔ (GBP(t) ∧ BD(t) ∧ OC(t))`.

No independence, decidability, consistency, or domain-specific meaning is assumed for `GBP`, `BD`, or `OC`.
