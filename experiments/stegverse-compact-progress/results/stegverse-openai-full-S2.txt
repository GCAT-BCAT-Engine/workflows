**Theorem formulation**

For every candidate transition \(t\):
\[
ALLOW(t)\iff GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t).
\]

Equivalently, the characterization consists of:

1. **Necessity**
   \[
   \forall t,\quad ALLOW(t)\to GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t).
   \]

2. **Sufficiency**
   \[
   \forall t,\quad GBP(t)\land BD(t)\land OC(t)\to ALLOW(t).
   \]

Under the stipulated evaluator assumption, both directions are respectively the forward and reverse implications of that assumption.
